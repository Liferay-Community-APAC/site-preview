Liferay Forums

Modern discussion forums for Liferay DXP. Members ask questions, share answers, and follow the conversations they care about, while moderators keep the community healthy.

Getting StartedWhat Liferay Forums includes and how to set up your first forum site. InstallingStep-by-step installation on Liferay Self-Hosted (Tomcat or Docker), or on Liferay PaaS or SaaS. Using the ForumStart discussions, reply, ask questions, vote, and follow topics. ModeratingLock topics, review reports, and ban members who break the rules. AdministeringManage categories, email notifications, the site's look, and several forum sites. ReferenceRoles and permissions, pages, configuration, and known issues.

Highlights

  • Discussions and questions. Post a topic as an open discussion, or as a question that can be marked as solved.
  • Categories. Organize topics into categories and one level of subcategories.
  • Mentions and subscriptions. Mention members with @, and get web and email notifications for topics you follow.
  • Voting. Upvote or downvote topics and replies.
  • Moderation. Members report content. Moderators validate reports, lock topics, delete topics, and ban authors per site.
  • Search. Find topics with facets for category, tag, author, and date.
  • Several sites. Run separate forums, each with its own categories, topics, and bans.
  • 13 languages. Labels ship in English, Arabic, Catalan, Chinese, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Hungarian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, and Swedish.

Supported Versions

Liferay Forums Liferay DXP
1.0.0 2026.Q1 LTS, with the required Liferay fixes. Tested on 2026.Q1.1 and 2026.Q1.4.
