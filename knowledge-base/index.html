Guides for apps and tools

User guides for the apps and tools we build for Liferay DXP: how to install them, use them and run them.

Unofficial resources. Use at your own risk.

The apps and guides here are shared by the Liferay Customer Success APAC team. They are not official Liferay products or documentation. Like the apps from Liferay Community on Liferay Marketplace, they aren't updated for every quarterly release and don't receive official Liferay support. Some are maintained only for certain quarterly releases, as needed. Test them thoroughly in a non-production environment before relying on them.

Best-effort support may be available for accounts with a Technical Account Manager (TAM) package, on a case-by-case basis. Contact your Success Delivery Manager for details.
