How the Liferay Customer Success team supports you

The Liferay Customer Success team for Asia Pacific sponsors this community and supports customers at every stage of their Liferay journey.

  1. 01

    Onboarding & adoption

    We help you get value from Liferay quickly. That means setting clear goals at kick-off, building a success plan around them, and supporting your teams as they adopt the platform.

  2. 02

    Technical advisory & health checks

    We review your architecture, configuration, performance and customizations against Liferay best practices, then give you a clear, prioritized plan for what to improve.

  3. 03

    Upgrades, migrations & lifecycle

    We help you keep up with Liferay's quarterly releases. We plan upgrades, migrate content from legacy platforms, and keep you on a supported, secure version.

  4. 04

    Enablement & knowledge sharing

    We share practical know-how about Liferay DXP as hands-on guidance, sample code and reusable tooling, so your own team can build with confidence.

  5. 05

    Partnership & measurable value

    We stay with you for the long term. Regular business reviews keep track of the outcomes that matter to you, and we connect you with the right Liferay experts across the region whenever you need them.

Unofficial resources. Use at your own risk.

The tutorials, sample code and guides here are unofficial examples shared by the Liferay Customer Success APAC team. They are not official Liferay products or documentation. Test them thoroughly in a non-production environment before relying on them.

SharePoint Online to Liferay DXP migration

A 14-chapter hands-on tutorial, with working tooling, for migrating a live SharePoint Online site, or SharePoint Server on premises, to Liferay DXP 2026.Q3 and its new CMS. MCP servers let an AI agent help inventory the source, plan the mapping and check the result, and a mock SharePoint lets you try it without a tenant.

Liferay Forums

Modern discussion forums for Liferay DXP

Discussion forums for Liferay DXP. Members ask questions, share answers and follow topics, while moderators keep the community healthy.

  • Discussions and questions. Post a topic as an open discussion, or as a question members can mark as solved.
  • Mentions and subscriptions. Mention members with @, and get web and email notifications for the topics you follow.
  • Moderation. Members report content; moderators validate reports, lock or delete topics, and ban authors per site.
  • Search. Find topics with facets for category, tag, author and date.
