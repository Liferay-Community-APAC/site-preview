Global community

Join the wider Liferay community online

Two places run by Liferay where users, customers, partners and Liferay employees around the world share what they know.

Liferay Discuss

discuss.liferay.com

Liferay's official community forum, open to everyone who uses Liferay or is interested in it: Free Tier users, customers, partners and Liferay employees.

  • Ask and answer questions in categories such as Development, Capabilities and General.
  • Shape the product: post and vote on Product Ideas, and give feedback on beta features and documentation.
  • Keep up with announcements and DEVCON news.

Questions about our Marketplace Apps, tutorials and tools? Post them in Development: see Get help.

Visit Liferay Discuss →

Liferay Developer Community

liferay.dev

Liferay's home for developers building on Liferay DXP.

  • Blogs from Liferay engineers and community members, with how-tos, release news and webinar replays.
  • Events such as DEVCON and community meetups.
  • Open source: how to contribute to Liferay's projects, and security advisories.
Visit liferay.dev →
